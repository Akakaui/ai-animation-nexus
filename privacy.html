Policy

Last updated: June 20, 2026

We take your privacy seriously. This policy explains exactly what data we collect, why we collect it, and how we protect it. We never sell your data.

Information We Collect

When you apply for AI Animation Nexus, we collect information you provide directly, including:

  • — Full name
  • — Email address
  • — WhatsApp number (optional)
  • — Portfolio or social media links (optional)
  • — Your goals and expectations for the course
  • — How you heard about us

How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • — Process your application and confirm your seat
  • — Send you your unique access code and session reminders
  • — Communicate with you about sessions, updates, and course logistics
  • — Track session attendance for the satisfaction guarantee
  • — Improve our services and future course content

Payment Information

All payment processing is handled securely by Paystack. We do not store, see, or have access to your credit card or payment details. Your payment information is processed directly by Paystack's PCI-DSS compliant infrastructure.

WhatsApp Communications

If you join our WhatsApp channel, you consent to receiving messages related to the course including session reminders, announcements, and community updates. You can leave the channel at any time without affecting your course access.

Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored securely on Vercel's infrastructure. We implement appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your personal information against unauthorised access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

Data Retention

We retain your information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide you services and fulfil our legal obligations. You may request deletion of your data at any time by contacting us.

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • — Access a copy of your personal data
  • — Correct any inaccurate information we hold about you
  • — Request deletion of your data
  • — Opt out of non-essential marketing communications

Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services: Paystack (payment processing), Resend / Nodemailer (transactional email), and Vercel (hosting and infrastructure). Each service operates under their own privacy policies.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or want to exercise your rights, please contact us at support@aianimationnexus.com or visit our Contact page.
